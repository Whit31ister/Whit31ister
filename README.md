# **`PANZERKAMPFWAGEN VI AUSF. B`**

> *"Known variously as the **Tiger Ausf. B**, **Tiger II**, **King Tiger**, or **Königstiger** (the British also referred to it as the 'Royal Tiger'). Exactly **489** Tiger IIs were produced."*

---

### **`01. TECHNICAL OVERVIEW`**

* **Classification:** Heavy Tank / Armored Fighting Vehicle
* **Designer / Manufacturer:** Henschel & Sohn
* **Production Period:** 1943 – 1945
* **Primary Armament:** 8.8 cm KwK 43 L/71 dual-purpose anti-tank gun
* **Operational Weight:** ~69.8 tonnes

---

### **`02. DESIGN & SPECIFICATIONS`**

* **Armor Thickness:** Up to 180 mm sloped front hull, providing exceptional defensive capabilities.
* **Firepower:** Designed to engage enemy armor at extreme ranges beyond return fire effective distances.
* **Engine & Powertrain:** V-12 Maybach HL 230 P30 gasoline engine delivering ~700 PS.
